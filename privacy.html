Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

This policy explains how Zatura (“we”, “us”) handles information collected through zatura.in.

What we collect

Only what you type into our contact form: your name, business email, company (optional), the service you need, budget range (optional) and your project description. We do not run advertising trackers or set tracking cookies on this site.

How we use it

To reply to your enquiry, prepare proposals and keep a record of our conversation. We do not sell your information.

Who processes it

The site is hosted on Vercel. Enquiries are delivered to us by a third-party form service, and fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when the page loads. These providers process data under their own policies.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to respond and to maintain a business relationship, then delete them on request or when no longer needed.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the information you sent us by emailing support@zatura.in.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
